Temporary CPT Program Pause

Washington University of Science and Technology (WUST) has temporarily paused its Curricular Practical Training (CPT) program until further notice.

WUST is not accepting or processing CPT applications or issuing CPT authorization during this period.

For questions, please contact CPT@wust.edu.

What is CPT?

Curricular Practical Training (CPT) is an F-1 employment authorization for eligible practical training that is an integral part of an established curriculum. CPT must be authorized by a Designated School Official (DSO) in SEVIS and reflected on the student’s Form I-20 before the authorized training begins.

What the Pause Means for Students

WUST is not accepting or processing CPT applications or issuing CPT authorizations during this period. Previously issued WUST CPT authorizations were updated to end no later than October 4, 2026. Students must not perform work or practical training requiring CPT authorization without valid authorization. Students must continue to maintain their F-1 status and applicable enrollment requirements.

Career Services

The Center for Career Services continues to provide career-development support, including resume assistance, interview preparation, and job-search guidance. Career Services assistance does not constitute employment authorization.
